Lynxcast

We got sick of running five different apps every stream, so we're making one. Channel points, sounds, TTS and alerts for Twitch, Kick and YouTube, all in one app on your own PC.

What's in it so far

RewardsTwitch and Kick channel points in one place. Swaps your rewards when you change game.
SoundsAny sound you want as a redeem. Got a link? Paste it in.
TTSChat gets read out on stream. Dodgy links and words get filtered, and there's a skip button for when chat gets creative.
OverlayOne browser source in OBS and everything shows up on it.
Redeem logSee every redeem live. Replay it, or refund it if it went wrong.
The restDeath counter, subathon timer, giveaways, song requests, multiview and a load more.

When?

Not yet. We're testing it on our own streams first, and we'll put it out once it's properly solid. Keep an eye on the streams below.

Your accounts

You log in to Twitch, Kick and YouTube inside the app, and those logins stay on your PC. There are no Lynxcast accounts and no tracking, and we never see your stuff. If you want the boring details, they're in the privacy policy.
