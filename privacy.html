Privacy policy

Lynxcast is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows PC. We don't run accounts, servers that keep your data, ads or analytics. This page explains exactly what the app touches and why.

The short version

  • Your Twitch, Kick and YouTube logins are stored only on your computer.
  • The app talks directly to Twitch, Kick and YouTube for you. We never see your data.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • You can remove everything at any time (see Deleting your data).

Who we are

Lynxcast is made by the streamer ColtKingS and a small team of streamers. Throughout this page, "we" means the people who make Lynxcast. You can reach us on Twitch at twitch.tv/coltkings, on Discord (ColtKingS), or with the Send feedback button inside the app.

What the app stores on your PC

The following is saved in the app's folder on your computer (%APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%) and never leaves it, except where described further down:

  • Sign-in tokens for the platforms you connect.
  • Your settings, rewards, collections, sounds and overlay layout.
  • A log of recent redeems and simple stream stats (viewers and chat activity), so you can see them in the app.

YouTube and Google

If you connect YouTube, Lynxcast asks Google for the youtube.force-ssl permission. It uses the YouTube Data API only to:

WhatWhy
Read your channel's ID and nameTo show which channel is connected.
Check whether you're live (your live broadcasts)To find your current live chat.
Read messages and Super Chats in your live chatTo respond to chat commands, run giveaways and show Super Chat alerts on your stream.
Post messages in your live chatTo reply to chat commands (for example !sounds), but only when you've turned those on.

This data is processed on your own PC while the app is running. It is not sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't used for advertising or to train AI models. Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer.

Lynxcast's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Lynxcast also uses the YouTube API Services, so by using its YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Twitch

With your permission, Lynxcast uses the Twitch API to create, update and pause your channel point rewards, see redemptions and mark them done or refunded, read and send chat messages, and read your follows, moderated channels, followers, subscriptions and Bits events. All of this happens from your PC directly to Twitch.

Kick

With your permission, Lynxcast uses the Kick API to manage your rewards, read redemptions and events, and send chat messages. Kick only sends events to a web address, so these events pass through a small relay we run on Cloudflare, which forwards them straight to your app and does not store them. The relay is also used to complete the Kick sign-in, because Kick requires a secret key that can't be kept inside a desktop app.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback, your message, your Twitch display name, the app version and your Windows version are sent to the developer's private Discord channel so we can read it and reply. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

Other services the app talks to

  • 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ: to show channel emotes.
  • MyInstants and YouTube: only when you paste a link to grab a sound. The audio is saved on your PC.
  • GitHub: to check for app updates.

These services see a normal web request from your PC, the same as visiting them in a browser.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or share your data. We don't use it for advertising, and we have no access to the data stored on your PC.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect a platform inside the app (Connect screen), or revoke access from the platform itself: Google account connections, Twitch connections or your Kick account settings.
  • Remove everything: uninstall Lynxcast and tick the box to delete app data, or delete the com.squadredeems.app folders in %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%.
  • Feedback messages: ask us and we'll delete any feedback you've sent.

Children

Lynxcast is for streamers who are old enough to use Twitch, Kick and YouTube under those platforms' own rules. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
