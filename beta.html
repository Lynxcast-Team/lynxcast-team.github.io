Get in early

Lynxcast isn't out yet, but we are letting streamers in early to test the app to polish it and help shape it. Made by streamers, for streamers.

How it works

1. ApplyFill in the form below with your Twitch or Kick account that you stream on.
2. We check it outWe look at every application ourselves, no AI is used to check applications.
3. You're inLog into Lynxcast with the account you submitted in the application and it unlocks. You get a beta tester number too.

Apply

We only use this to look at your application and get back to you. It is kept until the beta ends and will not be shared or sold. Details in the privacy policy.

Need to know

Lynxcast is a Windows app for Twitch, Kick and YouTube. Beta builds can and mostly will have bugs, that's the whole point. So tell us when something breaks. Your login stays on your PC like promised.
